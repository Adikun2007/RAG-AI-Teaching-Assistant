## Answer
Machine learning is a subset of AI that involves using data and sophisticated algorithms to make predictions or decisions. It's about understanding and arguing, and it includes supervised learning, where labeled data is used, as well as deep learning. Machine learning is used in various applications, including recommendations and predictions, and it encompasses a range of algorithms like regression, classification, decision trees, and clustering.
